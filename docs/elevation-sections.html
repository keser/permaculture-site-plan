Elevation Cross-Sections

Ground profiles through the lot, from the USGS 3DEP 1-meter DEM (NAVD88 feet). Vertical scale is exaggerated to make the slope readable. Two cuts: along the lot (street → back) and across it (west ridge → east low).

Section A — along the lot, Freestone Ave (S) → back (N)
~9× vertical exaggeration · the lot rises ~6 ft over ~550 ft ≈ 1% — imperceptible on the ground; the street end is the low end
Section B — across the lot at mid-length, west → east
~5× vertical exaggeration · a ridge crest ~240 ft west (119 ft) is the drainage divide; ground falls east past the lot to a ~90 ft low
