Site Water-Flow Schematic

Plan view of how water moves across the lot — from the 3DEP 1-meter DEM and the Lesson 4 zone map. A drafting guide for the Figma Site Water Flow Map (template p. 61) and Catchment Area Map (p. 59); trace it over the real base map.

Read: the ridge ~240 ft west is the divide — water arrives from the west / upslope side, crosses east-southeast + south, and exits at the SE corner + frontage to the Freestone Ave storm drains (≈100% today). The existing NW swale ⑤ holds roof water back toward the pool; a new SE-corner swale / rain garden ⑥ would sit in the outlet and catch the lot's whole throughput.
