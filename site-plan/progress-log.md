## 2026-09-18

### On-site 2026-09-12 through 2026-09-18 — piling, pruning, and a mushroom-log experiment; chipping deferred

- **Chipper (R4):** the clogged branch was cleared 2026-09-13 (Sunday night), but no chipping happened otherwise this week. Chipping is being deliberately held off a few weeks until Dad is available to help — it runs more efficiently with two people — which also buys the freshly cut material more time to dry before it goes through.
- **Branch piles organized for drying (R2/R3/R4):** most of the week's time went into building clean, organized piles of pine (spruce) branches to dry before breaking them down for the chipper. The largest main branches are too big to chip regardless of drying — once dry they'll go to outdoor firewood or a hugelkultur bed instead.
- **Maples (R8):** 5 maple trees felled total — a running total, up from the 4 logged 2026-09-05 (same felling project, not a separate batch). Branches removed and piled to dry; trunks will be chainsawed into firewood after a season of drying.
- **Mushroom log experiment started (S7 moves to In Progress):** before the rest of the maple wood goes to firewood, some 4"-diameter maple and mulberry logs were set aside, drilled, plugged with inoculated dowel spawn, and sealed with soy wax — now stacked under the pool deck in a shaded, moist spot. Both shiitake and oyster spawn were used, each in both wood species, as a deliberate comparison: the maple logs were fresh-cut green wood, the mulberry fresh-cut but already dead/dry. Open question the experiment is meant to answer: does wood freshness/species make a difference per mushroom species.
- **Invasives, another round (R1):** triclopyr applied again to Asiatic bittersweet, poison ivy, several other invasive ivy species, and Rose of Sharon sprouts — plus a newly found isolated Japanese knotweed plant. Separately, the entire tree- and ivy-covered fence line was cut back and sprayed; it's now dying back and drying, and will need mulching/composting in the next visit or two.
- **Lilacs (R21):** a major prune completed, beyond the lighter prune from 2026-09-05. No air-layering happened on the lilacs, cedar, or Rose of Sharon this visit — deliberately pushed to spring, since it's now too late in the season to start layers.
- **Cedar:** another cleanup pass, piled and ready for chipping whenever that resumes.
- **Grapes (R20) — plan change:** rather than digging the vines up now, decided to leave them in place until they go dormant. Marked with stakes and protected with a metal raised bed placed around them so they don't get disturbed by other work in the meantime.
- **Blueberries (D4) — plan change:** dropping the root-cutting propagation idea; sourcing new low-bush blueberry plants instead, to use as ground cover in Zone 5.
- **Mulch prep (R5):** enough cardboard collected to sheet-mulch the whole garden surface. Waiting on the deferred chipping pass before it can go down.
- **Compost (S2):** still not built — blocked on the same deferred chipping/shredding pass.
