## 2026-09-11

### Weekend of 2026-09-05 — chipper operational, first pass

- **Wood chipper up and running (R4):** first real chipping pass. Key finding: **wood
  needs to be as dry as possible — green leaf material bogs the shredder.** Cut branches
  and logs are now piled to dry before shredding instead of fed in fresh.
- **Zone 5 tree work:** dead mulberry branches cleared (R2). 4 maples cut under the
  mulberry, trunks saved for later reuse (R8 — moved to In Progress). Some, not all, of
  the Norway spruce low branches cut (R3) — an afternoon of cutting still remains.
  (**Correction:** R3's description said "Norway pines"; the actual species is Norway
  spruce, matching `site-profile.md` — fixed there and in the overview.)
- **Poison ivy, second round (R1):** triclopyr applied to a patch on the north side of
  the mulberry (Zone 5), and a deep spray along the Zone 3 fence line and the corner west
  of the chicken coop. Foliage should be mostly dead back by the next visit, opening that
  ground up for continued clearing.
- **Grapes (R20) and cedar/Rose of Sharon air-layers (R21):** grapes not yet moved —
  carried forward. Air-layers underway per the 2026-09-03 plan.
- **New idea — blueberry propagation (D4):** root cuttings from the existing blueberry to
  expand the planting instead of buying new stock. Not started; noted for later.
- **Design note — chicken coop fencing (R16):** the fenced garden area doubles as the
  dogs' free-roam space, so whenever the garden fence comes out, the coop needs its own
  standalone fenced perimeter. It does **not** need to reuse the old dog kennel as that
  perimeter's back fence.

### Looking ahead: on-site 2026-09-12 through 2026-09-18

- Trim the staged maple/spruce branches and logs to shredder-ready size (no Y-branching,
  no thick stumps), then shred them.
- Spread the resulting mulch in the Zone 3 veg garden, starting at the chicken coop and
  working outward, in a thick layer — over flattened cardboard laid first (sheet mulch).
- Continue Zone 4/5 clearing behind the now-dying-back poison ivy.

### Labor-estimate correction

Clearing Zones 4 & 5 is taking longer than expected — this visit's time went to maple
cutting, the second poison-ivy round, and prepping/staging debris for shredding rather
than clearing itself. **Zone 5 stays the priority** (it's where the new compost systems
are sited), but the original clearing timeline was optimistic; expect it to keep
competing with tree work and shredder prep for the next several visits.
